Honda is committed to reducing the carbon emissions from our
products and operations, as we work towards our vision of a
carbon-free society. In addition to our lineup of efficient hybrid
vehicles and the all-electric Prologue SUV projected to arrive early

agreements (VPPAs) for renewable wind and solar energy. When
operational, the electricity generated from these VPPAs will offset
60% of the electricity Honda uses in North America.
