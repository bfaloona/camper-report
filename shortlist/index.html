Shortlist

Edit anything it gets wrong under Criteria — the threshold and the comparison are editable, not just the name. Wants the data can't check land in Notes instead.

One click per trait: YES filters to vehicles that have it, NO rules them out, the middle state does nothing. Unknown data never rules a vehicle out — it shows as a ? badge instead. Each trait names its formula or source; the evidence lists are the report's own pros-and-cons bullets, kept as supporting text.

Things the data can't check — comfort features, hunches, anything to weigh yourself. These never affect the ranking, which is the point: a criterion that quietly scores nothing is worse than a note that never claimed to.
